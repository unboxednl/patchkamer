# mastering

*Nederlandse versie: [README_NL.md](README_NL.md)*

Devices to finish a mix: tone, dynamics, saturation, width, loudness and checking. All of them have **presets** for many music styles and for situations such as club, festival, big room, home, headphones, studio, radio and car.

| Device | What it does | Folder | Manual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Balance EQ** | Five-band parametric equalizer with dynamic bands and analysis suggestions | [`BalanceEQ/`](BalanceEQ/) | [Balance EQ](../../docs/mastering/USER-MANUAL_EN.md#balance-eq) |
| **Multiband Balance** | Three-band multiband compressor or broad dynamic EQ | [`MultibandBalance/`](MultibandBalance/) | [Multiband Balance](../../docs/mastering/USER-MANUAL_EN.md#multiband-balance) |
| **Bus Compressor** | Stereo-linked bus compressor with parallel mix and sidechain | [`BusCompressor/`](BusCompressor/) | [Bus Compressor](../../docs/mastering/USER-MANUAL_EN.md#bus-compressor) |
| **Patina** | Subtle harmonic saturation: tape, tube or neutral | [`Patina/`](Patina/) | [Patina](../../docs/mastering/USER-MANUAL_EN.md#patina) |
| **Stereo Space** | Two-channel diffusion for width and depth, with a width guard for mono | [`StereoSpace/`](StereoSpace/) | [Stereo Space](../../docs/mastering/USER-MANUAL_EN.md#stereo-space) |
| **Master Chain** | Tone, bus compression and a lookahead limiter, with LUFS and true-peak metering | [`MasterChain/`](MasterChain/) | [Master Chain](../../docs/mastering/USER-MANUAL_EN.md#master-chain) |
| **Mix Check** | Mono, side and reference checking with loudness and correlation | [`MixCheck/`](MixCheck/) | [Mix Check](../../docs/mastering/USER-MANUAL_EN.md#mix-check) |
| **Master Director** | Sets the whole chain for one of 32 music styles and 9 situations | [`MasterDirector/`](MasterDirector/) | [Master Director](../../docs/mastering/USER-MANUAL_EN.md#master-director) |
| **Listening Output** | Transparent listening output with a listening volume | [`ListeningOutput/`](ListeningOutput/) | [Listening Output](../../docs/mastering/USER-MANUAL_EN.md#listening-output) |
