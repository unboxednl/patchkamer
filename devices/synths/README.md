# synths

*Nederlandse versie: [README_NL.md](README_NL.md)*

Devices that make sound from notes. Both synths have **tunes** (a tune is the complete setting of one synth).

| Device | What it does | Folder | Manual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Duofone** | A synth with two oscillators that can play two pitches at once, a shared filter and amplifier, ring modulation, hard sync and sample & hold | [`Duofone/`](Duofone/) | [Duofone](../../docs/synths/USER-MANUAL_EN.md#duofone) |
| **Acid Baselines** | A bass synth with a 16-step sequencer (accent, slide and octave per step), 16 patterns, extra oscillators, a second filter and four kinds of distortion | [`AcidBaselines/`](AcidBaselines/) | [Acid Baselines](../../docs/synths/USER-MANUAL_EN.md#acid-baselines) |
