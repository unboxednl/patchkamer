# Changelog

*Nederlandse versie: [CHANGELOG_NL.md](CHANGELOG_NL.md)*

All notable changes to Patchkamer (the host and the desktop app) per version. The version number is in `patchkamer/version.json` and shown under **Settings > Version**. Older history is in the git log.

## 0.1.0 (not released yet)

### New
- **Drum Listener** (new device, Tools category): listens to a drum loop and writes down the pattern. Load an audio file (the Samples tab has a **→ Drum Listener** button for it), or record a few bars from the audio input, for example from Audio Capture. It finds the hits, sorts them into kick, snare (clap, rim), closed and open hat, toms and percussion, works out tempo, swing and grid (1/16, 1/32, triplets) and shows a lane grid you can edit. Send it with the clock to a drum machine (General MIDI notes or trigs), or copy it with the Copy button and paste it into the Pattern Tracker, Clip Arranger or Step Sequencer. Everything runs on your computer, with no online service. Measured on our own drum loops, not on real recordings and not listened to: kick and hats are recognised well, the snare fairly well; open hat, rim, clap, toms, percussion and anything with bass or chords under it are weak.
- **Own icon for every device.** Each of the 92 devices has its own line icon (`icon.svg` in its device folder), drawn for what the device does. The host shows it in the library, on the card and when minimised, in the colour of the category the device belongs to. The desktop app shows them too. Icons are not loaded from other sources given as a web address.
- **Master Director presets reach the devices.** When the Master Director's choice (style, situation and strength) is one of its own presets, for example after loading Situations › Club or pressing Set the chain, the connected mastering devices load their own preset with the same name, so it shows in their preset box and their settings are that preset's. Any other combination or strength still uses the Director's calculated values. Devices without such a preset get the calculated values; excluded devices and Mix Check are left as they are.
- **Samplers category in the library.** Samplebank and Loop Slicer now sit together in their own **Samplers** category (before they were under Synths and Drums & grooveboxes). It is part of the default library layout (`devices/patchkamer-bibliotheek.json`).
- **Controller presets for the Control Surface** (Control Surface 1.14.0). A preset now carries the MIDI assignments as well as the shape, and the new **Controllers** folder has presets for Ableton Push 2 and 3, Akai APC mini and APC mini MK2, Akai MPK mini MK3, Novation Launchpad Mini MK3 (Programmer Mode) and Launch Control XL (user template 1), Traktor Kontrol F1 (shape only) and a generic row of eight sound controllers. The numbers come from public documentation and are not tried with the hardware.
- **Woodwinds** (new device, Synths category): a woodwind family with its own engine: flute, clarinet, oboe, bassoon, alto and tenor saxophone, recorder and pan flute. Every voice is a harmonic series with an exact pitch whose overtones follow a model of the instrument (a slope that opens with the breath pressure, cylinder or cone, formants, overblow), with air noise through a comb tuned to the note, a tongue chiff on every note, vibrato that starts after a delay, legato glide for overlapping notes, bite and growl for the reeds, and one to three players per note. With a pack of 42 tunes (flute, clarinet, oboe, bassoon, sax, recorder, pan flute, section, ballad, jazz, folk, ambient). Not listened to yet.
- **Guitar** (new device, Synths category): a plucked-string family with its own engine: steel-string, nylon, clean electric, overdriven electric, palm-muted, 12-string and bass guitar. Every string is a small waveguide model with a pick position, a damping that follows how hard you play, string stiffness, a body or a pickup and amp; six strings with one voice each, chords spread over the strings with a strum helper, hammer-ons and pull-offs for overlapping notes, and pitch bend. With a pack of 40 tunes (acoustic, nylon, 12-string, clean, crunch, muted, bass, strum, ambient, funk, rock). Not listened to yet.
- **Piano** (new device, Synths category): an acoustic piano family with its own engine: grand, upright, honky-tonk, tine and reed electric pianos and a clavichord. Every note is a bank of decaying inharmonic partials with detuned strings, hammer noise, a velocity-dependent tone, a sustain pedal, a damper and a soundboard, placed in the stereo image by pitch. With a pack of 43 tunes (grand, upright, honky-tonk, tine, reed, clavichord, layered, ballad, pop, jazz, lo-fi). Not listened to yet.
- **Brass Section** (new device, Synths category): a polyphonic brass instrument with its own engine. A filter swells open on every note (the blat) and opens further for harder notes, with pitch scoop, breath noise, vibrato that starts after a delay, 1 to 4 detuned players per note and a mute. With a pack of 32 tunes (trumpet, trombone, horn, section, stabs, swells, funk, ballad). Not listened to yet.
- **Record and play the settings of a device** (contract v1.21). In the Clip Arranger you can turn the knobs of the connected device while a clip, a scene or the whole song plays, and the sequencer records every change as a line per control, next to the notes, which are left alone. A value stays until the next change. Settings moved by Drift, LFO or MSEG are recorded too. In a clip's new section **Settings** you see a line per control, flat until you turn. Per song you choose who plays them: the sequencer exactly as recorded (modulation on those controls is ignored) or the modulators on top of the line. New for devices that build on it: `host.watchParams`, `host.setParam`, `host.lockParams` and `host.paramsOf`.
- **Skins: the skin editor (layer 3).** A full editor window for skins: choose a device, add appearances from a fixed list (Standard, Compact, Wide, Phone, Touch, Custom), see the result live on a real card of the device (outside your workspace) at phone, tablet or desktop width with the screen class simulated, select controls, meters, pads, displays and panels by clicking the preview or the structure tree, change their properties with one form per kind (with which rule applies now and a rule for just that element), rearrange controls and panels by dragging (mouse and touch), undo and redo (at least 100 steps), a draft that survives a closing tab, live validation, save to your own library and export. The mini designer stays as a simple form. No device or skins contract change (skins 0.7, device contract v1.22).
- **Skins: panels and order.** A skin can now group the controls of a device in named **panels** (for example Oscillator, Filter, Output side by side) and choose their order; items are a control, a group, a role or *All others*. Per panel: title (Dutch and English), frame, direction, columns, spacing, bare controls, label position, alignment, width and *collapsible*. A skin hides nothing: whatever it does not mention lands in a rest panel. Sound, settings, presets and songs do not change and `host.controls` keeps the same keys; a device with a layout of its own (Control Surface) or without recognisable controls is skipped and the Skins tab says so. The mini designer has a **Panels** section under Controls. Skins contract 0.7, device contract unchanged (v1.22).
- **Skins: pads and display.** A skin can now give pads and step buttons another shape (round, square, rounded, rectangular), size, own colour per pad (device colour, neutral or a palette), an LED and feedback on press, when active and when a step fires; and it can show a choice list as the little screen of a physical device (LCD, OLED, segments or paper) with < > and optionally [ok], that you enlarge with a click into a panel with the whole list and a search field (also for the presets in the card head). Skins contract 0.6, device contract v1.22. The sound and settings do not change.
- **Chord Buddy** (new device, Tools category): helps you find chords without music theory. Pick a key and style, or play a chord on a MIDI keyboard: it recognises the chord (inversions, slash chords, incomplete chords), ranks the possible keys with how sure it is and why, suggests the next chord (safe, tension, colour), saves chords in 16 pads, plays the pads as a set (chords and bass on separate ports) and advises on bass lines and voice leading. Scales: major, minor (natural, harmonic, melodic), the seven modes, pentatonic and blues. A pack of 30 starter sets. Not listened to.
- **Audio Capture** (new device, Mixer category): brings the sound of another browser tab, a window or, on Windows, the whole system into the studio. One audio output that fits every audio input, so you can record, sample or process it. Mode, gain, high-pass, polarity, mute and meters; a pack of 12 presets. Nothing starts by itself: you choose the source in the share dialog of the browser. In the desktop app it shares the first screen and, on Windows, the sound of the whole system.
- **Skins: sections.** A skin can now give the controls of one group (for example the filter) a frame, lay them out in columns (3 columns = 3×3), make them bare (no frame of their own) and put the name under the control, and an appearance can set the card width so "Compact" makes the whole device smaller (skins contract 0.5; only when you pick the skin or appearance yourself, not with Auto). The mini designer has a Sections part and a Width field. Nothing is moved or hidden; sound and settings do not change.
- **Skins: meters.** A skin can now show a meter as a bar, an LED ladder or a round VU meter with a needle swinging from left to right, in the same place, with the length and width you choose (skins contract 0.4, device contract v1.18). The sound and settings do not change.
- **Desktop app** (Electron): Patchkamer in its own window, with the bundled library (devices, presets, tunes) and a portable Windows exe that needs no install. Built by the GitHub Action *App*. A folder `bibliotheek` next to the exe replaces the bundled library.
- **ASIO for the app**: audio can go straight to an ASIO driver for lower latency. Button **ASIO** at the bottom left, off by default. Choose driver, buffer, output pair, sample rate and margin; the button shows the estimated delay. Tested with an audio interface at about 17 ms.
- **Version number** under Settings: the version of Patchkamer, and in the app also the version of the app and the latest release on GitHub, with a download link. A link to this changelog.
- **Find sounds by instrument or style.** The Tunes and Presets tabs get **By instrument** and **By style**: an indented tree (Guitars → Nylon, Pianos, Brass, …; Hip-hop, Jungle, Techno, …) with search and a **Load** button that puts the tune on a card. The bundled tune and preset packs are tagged. New in the contract (v1.19): optional `tags` on tunes, presets, folders and packs.
- **Hip-hop songs and a folder tree in the Songs tab.** Four hip-hop songs in different sub-styles (boom bap, trap, lo-fi, G-funk) in `songs/hip-hop`, checked on paper and by measurement but not listened to. The Songs tab groups demo songs in subfolders under an indented heading.
- **Samples tab and break loops.** A new library tab for sample packs (`.patchkamer-samples.json`, contract v1.20): browse by style or by pack, listen with ▶ and load a sample into the Loop Slicer or the Samplebank (both get an optional `loadAudio`, version 1.20.0). The first pack, **Break Loops**, has fourteen two-bar drum loops (breakbeat, jungle, drum and bass, boom bap) rendered from our own drum devices; the Loop Slicer gets ten **Break Chops** tunes. Measured, not listened to.
- **Jungle, breakbeat and drum and bass songs.** Three songs that play a break from the Break Loops pack through the Loop Slicer: `songs/jungle`, `songs/breakbeat`, `songs/drum-and-bass`. Measured, not listened to.
